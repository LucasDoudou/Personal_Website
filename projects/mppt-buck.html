MPPT Photovoltaic Buck Converter

A complete photovoltaic-interface converter project spanning power-stage design, simulation, PCB implementation, firmware, experimental measurement, and loss optimization.

LinkedIn project / GitHub link ↗

Scope

Designed a maximum-power-point-tracking buck converter for interfacing with a photovoltaic module. The design supports a 16–24 V input range and provides a 12 V, 50–100 W output.

Implementation

  • Selected power-stage components and performed PLECS simulation.
  • Designed gate-driver and sensing circuitry.
  • Designed the PCB in Altium Designer, then soldered, brought up, and debugged the board.
  • Implemented an MPPT algorithm on a TI C2000 microcontroller.
  • Performed loss analysis and design optimization.

Measured result

The converter reached a measured efficiency of 95.49% with a switching frequency of approximately 80 kHz.

What this project demonstrates

End-to-end power-electronics development: analytical design decisions, simulation, mixed signal sensing/drive circuits, PCB layout, embedded control, hands-on debugging, and experimental efficiency measurement.
