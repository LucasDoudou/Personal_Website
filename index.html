About me

I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan. My current interests are centered on power electronics, converter modeling and control, wireless/capacitive power transfer, mixed-signal circuits, and embedded hardware.

Before Michigan, I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from ShanghaiTech University and was an exchange student at UC Berkeley. My work has ranged from analytical small-signal modeling and circuit simulation to PCB design, microcontroller firmware, Mixed Signal Integrated Circuits (Front-End and Back-End Design), and board-level embedded systems.

Research

Research Assistant, AEPCL Group, ShanghaiTech University · Supervisor: Prof. Minfan Fu · May 2024–Jun. 2026

Sep. 2025 – Jun. 2026

Undergraduate Thesis — Small-Signal Modeling of CPT Systems with CLL-CLL Compensation

Developed small-signal models for the inverter, rectifier, and resonant tank of double-sided CLL/CLLC-compensated CPT systems.

Constructed a 17th-order full-order equivalent circuit using sine–cosine decomposition and reduced it to a 5th-order model using L-to-C / C-to-L transformations, Thevenin/Norton equivalents, and approximation. Validated the reduced-order model in SIMPLIS.

Manuscript under review · APEC 2027
May – Aug. 2025

Large-Signal Modeling of a WPT System for High-Speed Motion Applications

Reproduced a modeling method from a doctoral thesis and developed a large-signal equivalent circuit for an LCC-S resonant converter with speed-dependent mutual coupling.

Reduced the model order and explored the mathematical relationship between time-varying mutual inductance and output response.

Sep. – Dec. 2024

Small-Signal Modeling of Resonant Converters with S-S and LCC-S Compensation

Studied resonant converters for wireless power transfer, including ZVS/ZCS soft switching and magnetic-component analysis.

Derived small-signal models, built equivalent circuits, and verified their accuracy using PSIM/SIMPLIS and MATLAB.

May – Sep. 2024

Closed-Loop Buck Converter Small-Signal Modeling

Modeled a PWM closed-loop buck converter and derived small-signal transfer functions around its steady-state operating point in MATLAB.

Built corresponding equivalent circuits in PSIM/SIMPLIS and validated the mathematical model through simulation and board-level experiments.
